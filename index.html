01 — Who We Are

A construction company that became a developer.

The AM Group was established as a privately owned construction company and has grown to developing and operating real estate. We leverage our construction and engineering expertise to undertake value-add real estate investment opportunities.

Our track record spans multifamily units, industrial space and residential condominiums — beginning with acquisitions in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and now diversifying our geographic presence across the United States.

06 — FAQ

Common questions.

The AM Group is a privately owned construction, development and real estate investment firm. We develop, own and operate multifamily, industrial and tech-flex real estate, leveraging our construction and engineering expertise to pursue value-add opportunities.

Our office is at 589 8th Avenue, 3rd Floor, New York, NY 10018. We are built in New York City and develop in select urban markets — with our portfolio concentrated in Pittsburgh and Zelienople, Pennsylvania.

Multifamily housing, industrial space, tech-flex campuses and for-sale residential condominiums. Current examples include The Stanza (multifamily, now leasing), 51 Bridge (tech flex, 100% leased to Westinghouse) and Zelienople Industrial Park (488,000 RSF).

The AM Group was founded in 2015 by Scott Wolkowitz and Tom Sabol, PE — combining five generations of New York City construction experience with licensed engineering and design expertise.

For investment, joint-venture, leasing or development inquiries, contact us at info@theamgrp.com or connect with us on LinkedIn.
